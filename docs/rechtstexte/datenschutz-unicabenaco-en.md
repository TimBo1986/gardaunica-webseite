### 2. Principle

This website sets **no cookies**, uses **no analytics or tracking services** and
embeds **no fonts, maps or scripts from third-party servers**. A consent banner
is therefore not required.

We process personal data only if you sign up for the waiting list yourself or
write to us.

### 3. Accessing the website and server log files

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P.
Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA — a Microsoft Corporation company.

When you access the site, your browser transmits technically necessary data which
GitHub processes in server log files: IP address, date and time, name of the file
retrieved, browser and operating system, and where applicable the previously
visited page.

This processing is necessary to deliver the website and ensure its stability and
security. The legal basis is our legitimate interest pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f)
GDPR. We ourselves have no access to these log files.

**Transfer to the USA:** GitHub also processes data outside the European Union.
This is based on the standard contractual clauses adopted by the European
Commission together with supplementary measures. Despite these measures, access
by US authorities cannot be entirely ruled out. Further information:
https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement

### 4. Waiting list

You can sign up to be notified when the app launches.

**What data we process**

- your name
- your e-mail address
- the website language you selected
- a randomly generated confirmation key
- the time of sign-up and the time of confirmation

**We do not store your IP address.**

**Confirmation procedure**

After signing up you will receive an e-mail with a confirmation link. Only after
you click it do we add you to the list. This rules out anyone entering someone
else's address.

**Legal basis**

Your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

**Withdrawal**

You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future — a message
to info@unicabenaco.com is sufficient. The lawfulness of processing carried out
until then remains unaffected.

**Purpose limitation**

We use your details solely to inform you about the launch of Unica Benaco and
directly related news. We do not pass them on to third parties and we do not sell
them.

**Storage period**

We store your details until you withdraw your consent. There is currently no
automatic deletion after a fixed period. At your request we delete your data
without delay.

**Services used**

- **Our own server** at Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestraße 25, 91710
  Gunzenhausen, Germany. The sign-up processing and the database holding the list
  run there. Data centre location: Germany. A data processing agreement pursuant
  to Art. 28 GDPR is in place.
- **Brevo** (Brevo SAS, 106 boulevard Haussmann, 75008 Paris, France) for sending
  the confirmation and notification e-mails. Brevo receives your e-mail address
  and your name already with the sending of the confirmation e-mail, that is,
  before your confirmation. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR
  is in place.

No transfer of your data to countries outside the European Union takes place in
connection with the waiting list.

### 5. Contact by e-mail

If you write to us, we process your details in order to handle your enquiry. The
legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the enquiry serves the initiation or
performance of a contract, and otherwise our legitimate interest pursuant to
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Our mailbox is operated by Zoho Corporation B.V., Beneluxlaan 4B, 3527 HK
Utrecht, Netherlands. Data is processed in data centres within the European
Union. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place.

We delete your enquiry once it has been dealt with, unless statutory retention
periods apply.

### 6. Language versions

Switching between German, Italian and English takes place in your browser. Your
choice is stored locally on your device so that the site appears in the same
language on your next visit. This storage is necessary for the operation of the
website you requested; no data is transmitted to us or to third parties.

### 7. No automated decision-making

No automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22
GDPR takes place.

### 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the data processed about you (Art. 15)
- **rectification** of inaccurate data (Art. 16)
- **erasure** (Art. 17)
- **restriction of processing** (Art. 18)
- **data portability** (Art. 20)
- **object** to processing based on a legitimate interest (Art. 21)

as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time.

A message to info@unicabenaco.com is sufficient to exercise these rights.

**Right to lodge a complaint:** You have the right to lodge a complaint with a
supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Garante per la
protezione dei dati personali, Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Rome, Italy
(www.garanteprivacy.it). You may also contact the supervisory authority at your
place of residence.

### 9. Changes to this policy

We will adapt this policy if the legal situation, our services or the technology
used changes. The version published on this page at any given time applies.
