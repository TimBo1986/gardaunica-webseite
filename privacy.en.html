4. Waiting list

You can sign up to be notified when the app launches.

What data we process

  • your name
  • your e-mail address
  • the website language you selected
  • a randomly generated confirmation key
  • the time of sign-up and the time of confirmation

We do not store your IP address.

Confirmation procedure

After signing up you will receive an e-mail with a confirmation link. Only after you click it do we add you to the list. This rules out anyone entering someone else's address.

Legal basis

Your consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Withdrawal

You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future — a message to info@unicabenaco.com is sufficient. The lawfulness of processing carried out until then remains unaffected.

Purpose limitation

We use your details solely to inform you about the launch of Unica Benaco and directly related news. We do not pass them on to third parties and we do not sell them.

Storage period

We store your details until you withdraw your consent. There is currently no automatic deletion after a fixed period. At your request we delete your data without delay.

  • Our own server at Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestraße 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. The sign-up processing and the database holding the list run there. Data centre location: Germany. A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR is in place.

Brevo receives your e-mail address and your name already with the sending of the confirmation e-mail, that is, before your confirmation. No transfer of your data to countries outside the European Union takes place in connection with the waiting list.
